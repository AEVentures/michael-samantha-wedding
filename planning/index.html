Venue Candidates

Researched August 2026. Confirm pricing and availability directly — 2028 rates will differ.

Important: August 8, 2028 falls on a Tuesday. St. Patrick's Cathedral celebrates weddings only on Saturdays — so keeping the exact 8/8/28 date rules it out (nearest Saturdays: August 5 or 12, 2028). The California venues host weekday weddings, often at lower rates.

1. Roaring Camp Railroads — Felton, CA (Santa Cruz Redwoods)

Steam train · Cathedral Grove fairy circle · Barn reception

An exclusive-use, 100-year-old steam train carries you and your guests through the forest to Cathedral Grove — a natural fairy circle of redwoods atop Bear Mountain — for the ceremony. Reception in Bret Harte Hall, a 6,120 sq ft redwood barn with wrought-iron chandeliers and twinkle lights, opening onto a lawn with fire barrel and s'mores. This is the "train in the redwoods" venue.

  • Capacity: 80–300 guests in the barn
  • Packages (2026 rates): Cathedral Grove w/ steam train ~$14,000 · Schoolhouse Lawn ~$12,000 · Barn ~$10,000 (catering additional; BYO beer/wine with no corkage)
  • Contact: roaringcamp.com · Wedding coordinator on staff · 5401 Graham Hill Rd, Felton, CA 95018
Fit: Celestial-and-earthy, woodland-fairy magic, the train Michael remembered. A ceremony inside a redwood fairy circle is deeply church-like — and a priest or pastor can officiate a Christian ceremony on site.

2. Nestldown — Los Gatos, CA (Santa Cruz Mountains)

36 acres · Redwoods, gardens, ponds & waterfalls · Tiny forest train

A private family-owned estate: redwood ceremony grove with ferns, lush gardens, two ponds with waterfalls, a barn with floor-to-ceiling windows and grand chandeliers — plus its own tiny train through the forest and an adult-sized playhouse. Described by its owners as a healing, restorative place.

  • Pricing (2026): $14,950–$39,500 depending on season/day (weekdays cheaper)
  • Tours: weekends by appointment · nestldown.com · 408-353-5311 · 22420 Old Santa Cruz Hwy, Los Gatos, CA 95033
Fit: The most "woodland fairy" of all — perfect for Samantha. More manicured and intimate than Roaring Camp; higher price band.

3. St. Patrick's Cathedral — New York, NY

Catholic sacramental ceremony · "America's Parish"

  • Requirements: at least one spouse baptized Catholic; residency in the Archdiocese of New York (or contiguous NYC diocese); initial interview at least one year before the date
  • Saturdays only — conflicts with Tuesday 8/8/28
  • Contact: weddings@saintpatrickscathedral.org
Note for your situation: Since you married civilly, a Catholic church ceremony is called a convalidation — a true exchange of vows in the Church, warmly encouraged by the Archdiocese. It requires meeting with a parish priest and a short preparation period. Any Catholic parish can do this — including a Florida parish near home, or a Catholic chapel in California — if St. Patrick's residency/date rules don't fit.

4. Mount of Olives — Jerusalem, Israel

Ancient ridge · Garden of Gethsemane · Panoramic view of the Old City

The Mount of Olives is one of the most spiritually charged places on earth — the ridge where Jesus prayed, wept over Jerusalem, and ascended. A wedding here would be less a traditional venue booking and more a pilgrimage ceremony: exchange vows overlooking the Old City walls, the Dome of the Rock, and the Church of the Holy Sepulchre in the distance, with the Garden of Gethsemane and the Church of All Nations a short walk away.

  • Ceremony options: Outdoor overlook, terrace of a church or guesthouse, or inside a Catholic/Christian chapel on the mount. A local Catholic priest or Protestant minister can officiate; a Catholic convalidation would need coordination with the Latin Patriarchate of Jerusalem.
  • Timing: Tuesday 8/8/28 is fine for a non-Saturday ceremony. August in Jerusalem is hot and dry (mid-80s°F, strong sun), so a sunrise or late-afternoon ceremony is wisest.
  • Logistics: Guests fly into Tel Aviv (TLV) and travel ~1 hour to Jerusalem. Plan 2–3 days of events for those making the journey. Plan on a very small core guest list for the Mount of Olives itself; a larger reception could happen in the U.S. afterward.
  • Considerations: Requires careful coordination with local clergy, possible permits, and sensitivity to the sacred sites. Not a "wedding vendor" venue — this is a spiritual expedition.
Fit: The most sacred and adventurous option. The mountains, the biblical weight, and the sunrise over Jerusalem fit the celestial, eternal theme of two infinities meeting in a holy place.

Recommendation

To keep the meaningful 08.08.2028 date and the sacred + woodland vision: hold the ceremony in the redwoods (Roaring Camp's Cathedral Grove or Nestldown) with a priest/minister officiating. If a formal Catholic convalidation matters, do it with your local parish first or that week, then celebrate big under the trees. Tuesday dates book easily and cost less — but tour both venues in 2026/early 2027 to lock the date. If the call is to make the wedding itself a pilgrimage, the Mount of Olives is the once-in-a-lifetime option — but plan it as a separate, smaller sacred ceremony with a U.S. celebration to follow.
