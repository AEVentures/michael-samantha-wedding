The Tapestry

The Signs We Steer By

∞

Every marriage gathers its own constellation of symbols. These are ours — the small holy things we navigate by.

🕊

The Hummingbird

Its heart beats twelve hundred times a minute, yet it hovers perfectly still — unhurried, sipping the sweetness of the moment. Love that works that hard, and rests that lightly.

∞

Eternity

Our date carries it twice: 08 · 08. An eight standing is a wedding ring atop a wedding ring; an eight resting is infinity. What God joins does not end.

🍞

Bread

Every feast in every age begins the same way — bread, broken and shared. Give us this day. To marry is to promise someone your daily bread, forever.

⚓

The Sea & The Navy

Navy is the color of the deep sea and the midnight sky — the same blue, above and below. Sailors have always steered by stars. We honor those who serve upon the water.

ᚠ

The Hidden Folk

In Iceland they bend roads around the elf churches — álfakirkjur — because some holy places are too old and too small to see, and you honor them anyway. Samantha has always known where the fairies live.

⚔

Saint Michael

The Archangel — Michael's namesake — captain of the heavenly host, defender at the hour of need. Over this marriage, he stands watch.
